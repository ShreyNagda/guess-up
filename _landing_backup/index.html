How To Play

Easy rules to learn in less than 30 seconds. Perfect for players of all ages!

1

1. Select Decks & Align

Pick one or more theme decks. Select your timer duration, then hold the phone up to your forehead facing your friends.

2

2. Got it Right? Tilt Down

If you guess the word based on clues, tilt the screen down toward the ground to score 1 point and load the next word.

3

3. Unsure? Tilt Up

Stuck on a difficult word? Simply tilt the screen upwards to pass. No penalty is given, saving precious time!
